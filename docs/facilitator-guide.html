Step 2

Connect and start

Give students two things

  1. The website: motorcity.boeingcenter.com
  2. The six-character join code shown on your screen.

Students choose Join, enter the code and their name, then press Join session.

When a timer expires

1
The student's timer reads Time up.
2
Remaining materials allocate automatically.
3
The board locks. The student presses Advance round.

Each student has an independent timer. Refreshing or rejoining does not reset it.

Keep the reveal intact: Live standings use revenue only. Students do not see WIP rates, monetary penalties, or projected final scores during play.

Step 4

Finish and reveal

1
Open Private scoring. Check Score up to round and WIP penalty round.
2
Press End production, then Yes, end it.
3
On the projector, press Charge the unfinished cars when you are ready for the reveal.
4
Download Excel workbook.

What the workbook contains

  • Game Details: setup, resource sequence, scoring rounds, timer, economics, and notes.
  • Player Overview: revenue, penalty, final score, throughput, WIP, and identifier.
  • Game Stats: low, high, median, and mean for the class.
  • One sheet per player: every round, station, and resource movement.

Help

If something goes wrong

My browser closed

Choose Rejoin. Enter the join code, your facilitator name, and your recovery code.

A student's browser closed

The student chooses Rejoin with their own recovery code. Their board and timer return.

A student lost the code

On that student's card, press New code for [name]. Read the new code to the student.

Someone joined by mistake

Press Remove, then confirm. Removal cannot be undone after the run ends.

The screen says offline

Pause and wait for synced. Do not repeat a command rapidly.

We need to end early

Lower both scoring fields to a round the class reached, then end the run.

Most important: Never share the facilitator recovery code with students. Download the Excel workbook before leaving the finished session.
