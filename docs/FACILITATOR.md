# Running a class session

Everything below happens in a normal web browser. Nothing to install, for you or the students.

**Address:** https://motorcity.boeingcenter.com

---

## Before class

Nothing to prepare. The session takes about a minute to set up once you are in the room.

If you want the join code on the projector, plan to have two windows open: one on the projector
and one on your own screen. The section [Two screens](#two-screens) explains how.

---

## 1. Open the session

1. Go to the address above.
2. Under **Team session**, choose **Facilitate**.
3. Fill in **Facilitator name**.
4. Under **Active models**, tick the car models you want in play. Under **Resource plan**,
   choose *Classic demo sequence* (the original fixed sequence) or *Random 100 rounds*.
5. Press **Create session**.

You now have two codes:

| Code | What it is | What to do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Join code** | Six characters, e.g. `ZD2VBF` | Read it out or project it. Students need it. |
| **Recovery code** | Long, e.g. `77ScH4_WI6qN_NPeCYtohXYo` | Keep it. It is the only way back in if your browser closes. |

> Save the recovery code somewhere you can reach in class — an email to yourself is enough.
> Without it, a closed tab means a lost session.

---

## 2. Get the students in

Tell the class the address and the join code.

Each student:

1. Goes to the same address.
2. Under **Team session**, leaves **Join** selected.
3. Enters the **join code**, their **name**, and optionally a **student ID or email**.
4. Presses **Join session**.

The student ID is optional but worth asking for — it appears next to their name in the results,
which makes matching the export to your gradebook straightforward.

Names appear on your roster as students arrive. Nobody can move anything yet.

---

## 3. Start

When enough people have joined, press **Start production**.

Every student's factory opens at the same moment. From here they play their own factory; they
do not share a board.

---

## 4. During the game

Your screen shows:

- **Leaderboard** — live standings, with revenue, unfinished cars, the penalty, and the score
- **Factory floor** — a card per student, showing where their cars are sitting
- **Control** — the two settings below, and the button to end the run

Two settings control scoring. You can change them at any point while the game is running:

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Score up to round** | Revenue counts through this round. Later rounds are ignored. |
| **Charge unfinished cars at round** | Cars still on the floor at this round cost money. |

The penalty round cannot be later than the round you score up to; the screen will tell you if
you try.

### If a student gets stuck

- **Their tab closed, or their laptop died** — they press **Rejoin**, and enter the join code,
  their name, and their recovery code. Their factory comes back exactly as it was.
- **They lost their recovery code** — on their card in the Factory floor, press
  **New code for [name]**. A code appears at the top of your screen. Read it to them, then
  press **Done**.
- **Someone joined by mistake, or twice** — press **Remove** on their card, then confirm.

---

## Two screens

Press **Present** for a clean, large screen meant for a projector.

Before the game starts it shows the address, the join code in very large type, and the names of
everyone who has joined — so latecomers can get themselves in without interrupting you.

Once play begins it becomes a simple standings board: place, name, turn, and revenue. Nothing
else. The penalty arithmetic, unfinished-car counts, and scores stay on your screen only.

Press **Escape**, or **Close**, to leave it.

To use two screens at once, open the game in a second browser window, use **Rejoin** with your
own name and recovery code, and put that window on the projector. Press **Present** there.

---

## 5. End the run

Press **End production**, then **Yes, end it**.

Scores lock at the round you chose, and students can no longer move cars. They keep seeing their
own final board and summary.

You can still download the results afterwards — ending the run does not close anything off.

---

## 6. Get the results

Two buttons sit at the top of the Factory floor:

- **Excel workbook** — the one to use. It opens directly in Excel and contains:
  - *Player Overview* — one row per student: revenue, penalty, score, unfinished cars,
    peak and average work in progress, throughput, and their student ID
  - *Game Stats* — low, high, median, and mean across the class for each of those measures
  - *one sheet per student* — every round they played, where each car sat at each station, and
    which resources they were issued, exchanged, and left unused
- **CSV** — a single flat table, for pasting somewhere else

Both work after the run has ended. Download them whenever you like; the session is kept for
12 hours.

---

## Things worth knowing

- **The rules are the original game's.** Recipes, timings, paint booth capacity, the four-for-one
  exchange, and the economics all behave exactly as they always have.
- **Students never see each other's numbers** unless you project them. Their own screen shows
  their own factory only.
- **The standings order is the real score** — revenue minus the cost of unfinished cars. A
  student with more revenue can sit lower, because cars left on the floor are charged for. That
  is the point of the game, and it is worth saying out loud the first time it happens.
- **A session lasts 12 hours**, then it is cleared. Download the workbook before you leave.
- **Closing your browser does not end the game.** Students keep playing. Use your recovery code
  to get back in.
